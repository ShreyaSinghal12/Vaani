What we check against

Vaani does not guess from the wording of a message. It compares the message with this list of each bank's official websites, SMS sender names and helplines. Every entry has the page we took it from and the date we checked it.

Entries marked "to confirm" came from a secondary source or from real messages on our own phones. We confirm them on the bank's own site before relying on them.
